Privacy Policy

Applies to: Andy AI Workspace · Effective date: 27 September 2026

Who uses this app

Andy AI Workspace is a personal-use tool. The only users are the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What Google user data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app may access the owner's own Gmail messages and labels, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Sheets and Google Docs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out tasks the owner explicitly asks the locally running AI assistant to do (for example: summarize today's calendar, find or label emails, read or update the owner's spreadsheets). Content the assistant reads may be processed by the AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to fulfil the owner's request.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app runs no public server and keeps no separate database of Google user data.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties for their own purposes, not used for advertising, and not used to train generalized AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Andy AI Workspace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
