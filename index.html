andy-claude-workspace

andy-claude-workspace is a personal-use tool. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, and is not offered to anyone else.

It lets an AI assistant running locally on the owner's computer (Claude Code, connected through the open-source workspace-mcp server) work with the owner's own Google account data — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs — to carry out tasks the owner asks for, such as summarizing the day's schedule, finding emails, labelling mail, and reading or updating the owner's own spreadsheets.

Google account access is requested only so the owner can perform these tasks on their own data. No one else's data is collected, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
